Meals

Yogurt bowl~380 cal, 40 g protein
1.5 cups nonfat Greek yogurt, 1 cup berries, 1 tbsp chia
Overnight oats~380 cal, 35 g protein
½ cup oats, 1 cup Greek yogurt, ½ cup berries, splash of soy milk
Cottage cheese plate~370 cal, 40 g protein
1 cup cottage cheese, 1 cup pineapple or peaches, 1 hard-boiled egg
Big salad~650 cal, 45 g protein
Greens, cucumber, tomato, pepper, 6 oz smoked tofu, ½ cup chickpeas, ½ avocado, 1 tbsp olive oil and vinegar
Egg & hummus plate~650 cal, 42 g protein
2 hard-boiled eggs, ¼ cup hummus, raw veggies, 1 small whole-grain pita, ½ cup yogurt
Chickpea-feta bowl~600 cal, 38 g protein
1 cup chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, 1 oz feta, 1 tbsp olive oil and lemon, ½ cup yogurt
Veggie plate~650 cal, 48 g protein
¼ cup hummus, 1 cup edamame, 1 lentil pouch, 1 oz feta, raw veggies, 1 fruit
Tofu plate~620 cal, 45 g protein
6 oz smoked tofu, cucumber-tomato-pepper salad, ½ cup chickpeas, ½ avocado, olive oil and lemon
Tzatziki plate~650 cal, 50 g protein
1 cup Greek yogurt with grated cucumber and garlic, 2 hard-boiled eggs, raw veggies, 1 small pita, a few olives

Reminders

Sunday prep (20 min): boil eggs, rinse chickpeas, chop veggies, portion yogurt.
Ride days: add carbs during rides over 90 min, plus a planned 600–700 cal post-ride meal. Target 2,300–2,500 cal, then stop grazing.
Sodium: measure feta, olives, and hummus, and rinse canned beans.
Check progress with the weekly average of morning weight. If flat after 2–3 weeks, trim about 200 cal from oil, avocado, or the optional snack. Get labs (lipids with ApoB, vitamin D, B12, A1C) and review with your doctor. This is not medical advice.
